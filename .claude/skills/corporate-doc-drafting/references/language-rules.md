# Language Regime

Source: LOIE Guidelines Rev 04, §6 and §7.2.1.

## 1. Hierarchy

**English is the official language of Document Control and prevails over all other languages in the
event of discrepancy.** Russian and Arabic are recognised **working** languages, usable in
correspondence with the parent group and with Iraqi state authorities respectively.

This hierarchy is not decorative — it decides which text governs in a dispute. Never issue a
bilingual document without stating which version prevails.

## 2. Language by destination

| Destination | Language regime |
|---|---|
| State authorities of Iraq | Normally **bilingual English–Arabic**. Arabic-only is permitted when addressed directly to state departments. |
| National Oil Company (TOC / BOC) | Bilingual English–Arabic **or** English-only. The Document Owner decides. |
| Parent company / Group | Bilingual English–Russian **or** Russian-only. |
| All other organisations | English. |
| Executive documents (ORD) | Drafted, approved and registered in **English**. A Russian version may be attached where necessary. |
| Local regulations | Drafted, approved and registered in **English**. A Russian version may be attached where necessary. |

## 3. The prevalence clause

Any bilingual letter to the NOC must close with, verbatim:

> "This document is provided in Arabic for informational purposes only; the English text shall
> prevail in case of any discrepancies."

## 4. Arabic — outgoing

- Arabic outgoing letters **shall not be registered** without translation or proofreading by the
  Operator's translator.
- The **translator's confirmation** is attached by the Initiator to the registration request. Treat
  the missing confirmation as a blocking defect, not a formality.
- **Exception:** regularly recurring letters with standard text (blood tests, visa issues, work
  permits and similar) may be issued in Arabic only, without bilingual format, at the Document
  Owner's decision.

## 5. Arabic — incoming

- Incoming Arabic letters go to the Translator **before** registration and transfer to the recipient.
- Letters **up to three (3) pages** are translated in full.
- Longer documents may be accompanied by a **brief annotation**, then registered and forwarded; the
  Document Executor decides, on the basis of the annotation, whether full or partial translation is
  needed.

## 6. Other languages — incoming

Responses are given in the language of the original correspondence where feasible. Where not:
- **Russian** for letters received in CIS national languages;
- **English** for all other languages.

## 7. Forms of address

Full names are used when addressing individuals. Otherwise:
- `Dear Sirs` — to organisations in general
- `Dear Sir` — to a man
- `Dear Madam` — to a woman

## 8. Terminology discipline

- Only **official** abbreviations, terms and titles — organisations, countries, business units, job
  titles, units of measure.
- Company name rendering:
  - English: **no quotation marks** — `PJSC LUKOIL`
  - Russian: **quotation marks** — `ПАО «ЛУКОЙЛ»`
- **Proper names, including geographical names, are translated:**
  `LUKOIL Overseas Egypt Ltd.` → `ЛУКОЙЛ Оверсиз Египет Лтд.`
- **All other words are transliterated:**
  `LUKOIL Overseas Iraq Exploration B.V.` → `ЛУКОЙЛ Оверсиз Ирак Эксплорейшн Би-Ви.`

## 9. Entity naming — use the right label for the right audience

The same legal entity carries three different labels depending on who is being addressed. Getting
this wrong is a substantive error, not a stylistic one:

| Label | Used in |
|---|---|
| **Asset** | Correspondence within the group of companies |
| **Company** | Local regulations shared with Contractors, and official correspondence with Contractors |
| **Operator** | Correspondence with the NOC, Iraqi Ministries, and with Contractors when the status under the service contract must be emphasised |

## 10. Bilingual layout

Bilingual documents are legitimately executed as **two-column tables** — this is expressly permitted
alongside questionnaires and column breakdowns. Keep paragraph numbering identical across both
columns so that cross-references resolve in either language.
